A · Current — three typefaces

Catholic Scholar & Consultant

The Recovery of Unity

Andrew Likoudis is a Catholic scholar, editor, and consultant working at the intersection of theology, publishing, and Church communications. As Founder, Chairman, and President of the Likoudis Legacy Foundation, he continues the ecumenical and apologetic legacy of his grandfather, James Likoudis, in service of Catholic–Orthodox reconciliation.

His editorial work spans more than a dozen volumes on ecclesiology and the papacy, including the third edition of Ending the Byzantine Greek Schism, with a foreword by Scott Hahn. He holds an M.A. in Catholic Studies from Franciscan University of Steubenville.
